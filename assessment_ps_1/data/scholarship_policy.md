# Merit Scholarship Policy (Sample College Policy)

The following are illustrative sample college policies for this project. They
are not real institutional rules. Students should confirm actual eligibility
and application requirements with their institution.

- The sample merit scholarship requires a CGPA of 9.0 or above.
- The sample scholarship amount is Rs. 25,000 per academic year.
- No standing arrears are allowed at application time under this sample
  policy.
- The sample application deadline is before 31 August.
- The sample renewal CGPA requirement is 8.5.
- Meeting the CGPA threshold alone does not confirm all eligibility
  requirements; arrears and deadline requirements must also be met.
