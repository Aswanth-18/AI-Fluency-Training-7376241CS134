# AI Fluency Training Programme (Sample Information)

This is sample programme information for the helpdesk exercise and is not an
official announcement from a real institution.

## Programme information

The sample AI Fluency Training programme introduces learners to practical uses
of AI tools, responsible prompting, and applied project work. Participants
should follow the schedule and directions published by their own programme
coordinator.

## Daily practical tasks

Each training day includes a practical task. Learners should complete the
assigned exercise, save their work in the appropriate project folder, and
review any instructions supplied for that day. The specific task and deadline
should be confirmed with the programme coordinator.

## Repository submission

For this sample programme, learners should keep their assessment files
organized in the assigned repository folder and submit the repository URL
through the channel specified by their coordinator. Do not include passwords,
API keys, or other secrets in a repository.

## Contact information

Contact the programme coordinator or training support desk using the contact
details provided through the learner's official course channel. No real
telephone number or email address is specified in this sample document.
