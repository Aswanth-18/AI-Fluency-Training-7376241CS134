# Fee Policy (Sample College Policy)

The following are illustrative sample college policies for this project. They
are not real institutional rules. Students should confirm actual amounts and
deadlines with their institution's finance office.

- The sample annual tuition fee due date is 15 July.
- The sample late fee is Rs. 100 per day.
- The maximum sample late fee is Rs. 2,000 per semester.
- Scholarship eligibility depends on the published scholarship conditions.
