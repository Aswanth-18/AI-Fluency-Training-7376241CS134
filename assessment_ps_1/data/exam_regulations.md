# Examination Regulations (Sample College Policy)

The following are illustrative sample college policies for this project. They
are not real institutional rules. Students should confirm eligibility with
their examination office.

## Attendance and examination eligibility

- The sample minimum attendance requirement is 75%.
- Students with attendance from 65% up to, but not including, 75% may apply for
  condonation.
- The sample condonation fee is Rs. 500 per course.
- Students with attendance below 65% are not eligible to write the examination
  under this sample policy.
- Students should confirm their individual examination eligibility with the
  examination office.
