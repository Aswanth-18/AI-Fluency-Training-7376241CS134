# Library Rules (Sample College Policy)

The following are illustrative sample college policies for this project. They
are not real institutional rules. Contact the librarian for clarification.

- Sample working-day library timings are 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
- Sample Saturday library timings are 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM.
- The sample late return fine is Rs. 2 per day per book.
- Contact the librarian about lost or damaged books.
